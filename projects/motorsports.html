Controls

My work with MIT Motorsports focused on vehicle dynamics and controls for the Formula SAE program. A core modeling tool is the Pacejka tire model, which provides a practical nonlinear approximation of tire forces as slip conditions change.1 1. Team site: MIT Motorsports. This model is widely used when building controllers, validating simulations, and reasoning about how setup changes affect grip across the operating envelope.2 2. Background on the model: Hans B.
